1. Who we are

ORVANA FinEd ("we", "us", "our") is a mutual fund distributor registered with AMFI (ARN: [YOUR-ARN]). We operate the website orvanafined.com and provide financial awareness and mutual fund distribution services to retail investors in India.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"), we are a Data Fiduciary — meaning we determine the purpose and means of processing your personal data and are responsible for its protection.

We do not provide investment advisory services. We are a mutual fund distributor. Mutual fund investments are subject to market risks. Please read all scheme-related documents carefully before investing.

3. How we use your data

We process your personal data only for the following lawful purposes:

  • KYC compliance — PAN is collected as required under PMLA 2002 and SEBI/AMFI regulations for mutual fund transactions
  • Account management — to create and maintain your investor portal account
  • Portfolio disclosure — to show you your investment details in your dashboard
  • Communication — to send statements, NAV updates, and regulatory communications
  • Legal compliance — to comply with SEBI, AMFI, Income Tax, and PMLA obligations

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling, or sell it to any third party.

4. How we protect your data

4.1 Technical measures

  • Encryption in transit — all data transmitted over HTTPS/TLS 1.3
  • Encryption at rest — database encrypted at storage level (AES-256)
  • PAN protection — PAN numbers are hashed and masked; full PAN is never displayed or stored in readable form after initial submission
  • Row-level security — each client can only access their own data; database policies enforce this at query level
  • Access control — administrative access requires separate authentication; client keys cannot access other clients' data

4.2 Organisational measures

  • Only authorised personnel can access client data
  • Data access is logged and auditable
  • No client PAN or financial data is stored locally on any employee device

Despite our best efforts, no internet transmission is 100% secure. If you suspect any unauthorised access to your account, contact us immediately at grievance@orvanafined.com.

5. Data sharing and disclosure

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share data only in these limited circumstances:

  • AMCs and RTAs — fund houses and registrars as required to process mutual fund transactions (e.g. CAMS, KFintech)
  • Regulatory bodies — SEBI, AMFI, Income Tax Department when legally required
  • Infrastructure providers — Supabase (database, hosted in Mumbai, India) for data storage under strict data processing terms
  • Legal requirement — if required by court order or law enforcement with valid legal process

7. Your rights under DPDP Act 2023

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Right to correction — request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data
  • Right to erasure — request deletion of your data (subject to regulatory retention requirements)
  • Right to grievance redressal — raise a complaint with our grievance officer
  • Right to nominate — nominate another person to exercise rights on your behalf in case of death or incapacity

To exercise any of these rights, email grievance@orvanafined.com with subject line "DPDP Rights Request". We will respond within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may escalate to the Data Protection Board of India once it is constituted under the DPDP Act.

8. Cookies
